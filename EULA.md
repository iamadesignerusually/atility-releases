## Before distribution or Pro sales

The following is a maintainer checklist, not additional end-user terms:

- Have counsel review ownership, contributor permissions, employment-related
  rights, and AI-assisted code and asset provenance and protectability,
  the first release subject to these terms, permitted installations, consumer
  rights, warranty, liability, termination, and the enforceability and language
  of these terms in the intended markets.
- Audit earlier distributed copies and any prior license or contract grants.
  Preserve rights and required notices for reused material. Absence of a
  repository license file is not evidence of an MIT grant or proof that users
  have no existing rights.
- Keep the reviewed terms publicly accessible and include them with the app
  or download. The build copies the canonical root LICENSE and EULA.md into

  The public atility-releases repository carries the same documents. Keep those
  public copies in sync when these terms change. Present the reviewed terms and
  obtain agreement where required; the reader and public links do not implement
  agreement or approve this draft for distribution.

  disclosures for the actual app, Claude, and iCloud data flows, including
  the free edition. This EULA is not a privacy notice.
- Before enabling Pro, agree the commercial terms and provide verified
  seller/contact information, required purchase and withdrawal information,
  and a working payment and license system. Review any applicable electronic
  withdrawal-function requirements for online sales. Review any transition affecting
